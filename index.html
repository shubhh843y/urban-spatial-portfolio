Question 2: Ludhiana Night-time Radiance Trends (2014–2024)

City & Dataset: Ludhiana, Punjab | VIIRS Night-time Lights (NOAA/VIIRS/DNB/MONTHLY_V1/VCMSLCFG) | Boundary Source: OpenStreetMap (via osmnx / Nominatim)

1. Dynamic Temporal Animation (2014–2024)

The annual mean night-time radiance evolution visualizes the outward expansion of urban light intensity into peripheral zones over the 11-year observation period.

2. Long-Term Trend & Statistical Summary

Statistical Findings:

  • Linear Trend Slope: +0.17 nW/cm²/sr per year (R² = 0.84)
  • Total Percentage Change (2014–2024): +73.7% overall increase in city-wide mean radiance

Change Metrics: The mean pixel change registered at +1.62 nW/cm²/sr. Spatial analysis indicates that 85.9% of city pixels experienced a significant increase in brightness (>0.5 nW/cm²/sr), while only 0.9% dimmed.

4. Discussion & Limitations

Spatial Pattern: High radiance values are heavily clustered within the central commercial and industrial cores of Ludhiana. The change map reveals prominent outward radiation into peri-urban fringes, confirming active suburban development and industrial spillover.

Temporal Change: The linear regression trend (+0.17 units/year) and a 73.7% total surge reflect robust local economic growth, rural-urban transition, and increased rural electrification/infrastructure development.

Data Limitations: VIIRS night-time lights operate at a coarse resolution (~464 m per pixel), leading to a "blooming effect" where bright urban light spills over into adjacent unlit or dark rural pixels. Furthermore, night-time radiance serves as a robust proxy for economic activity and infrastructure but does not directly measure population density, wealth distribution, or precise energy consumption without complementary demographic datasets.
